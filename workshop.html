Arun | Stekelbaars

Lessons from highest-level political campaigns

by Arun Chaudhary

Tailored for CMOs and communications leaders. The program covers a wide range of international examples, while also analyzing the participants' own work and providing constructive feedback.

We explore the dynamics that determine whether or not a message resonates with the public. Even after the workshop concludes, participants continue to receive guidance and support in refining their messaging.

The workshop is hosted by Buro Stekelbaars and Arun Chaudhary. Arun worked in countless international campaigns. He was creative director for Bernie Sanders, President Obama’s first videographer and most recently creative advisor for the winning Democratic senate campaign in Mississippi.

This workshop teaches you the following

  1. How to navigate from ideal to message to change
  2. Arun takes you through his experience and vision
  3. We dissect countless (international) examples
  4. You get to work yourself. You draft your own message, based on your ideals, and we process that into a narrative (how that manifests on socials / speeches / PR etc.)
  5. You practice with experts, receive feedback, and can get to work immediately afterwards
