This workshop teaches you the following

  1. How to navigate from ideal to message to change
  2. Arun takes you through his experience and vision
  3. We dissect countless (international) examples
  4. You get to work yourself. You draft your own message, based on your ideals, and we process that into a narrative (how that manifests on socials / speeches / PR etc.)
  5. You practice with experts, receive feedback, and can get to work immediately afterwards
